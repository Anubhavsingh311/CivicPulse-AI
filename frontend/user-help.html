Municipal Ticket Guidance

How your report moves from intake to verification

1. Automated Semantic Clustering

CivicPulse groups co-located complaints (e.g. repeated reports of water outage in Sector 12). When a cluster reaches critical density, the incident is flagged on the city operations command dashboard as an emerging emergency, ensuring expedited triage.

2. Status Pipeline Definitions
Open

Logged in the database. Awaiting initial inspector assignment.

In Progress

Maintenance or sanitation crew assigned and on site.

Resolved

Work completed and signed off by municipal inspector.

Escalated

Requires inter-departmental budget approval or structural rebuild.

3. Service Level Agendas (SLAs)
  • Street Lighting: 24–48 hours for bulb replacement or fuse fixes.
  • Waste Collection: 24 hours for missed pickups or bin clearance.
  • Potholes & Road Damage: 48–72 hours for temporary cold-mix patch.
  • Water Contamination / Leaks: Priority response within 6–12 hours.
